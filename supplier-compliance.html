Supplier Compliance — User Guide

Make sure a vendor is qualified — and their certificates are still valid — before you confirm a purchase order. Written for a new team member.

1. What is this?

This module adds a compliance layer to Odoo's purchasing flow. Each product can require a minimum supplier certification, and each vendor is given one or more supplier levels. When you try to confirm, email, or print a purchase order, Odoo checks whether the vendor's level actually covers what the products require. It also watches vendor certificates for expiry, and can warn you — or block the order — when one has lapsed.

Why it exists: procurement teams need to avoid ordering products from suppliers who aren't qualified, or whose quality certificates have expired. Doing this from memory doesn't scale and leaves no audit trail. This enforces the policy at the moment of purchase and records every exception.

Two independent checks run on the purchase order:
1) Product vs. vendor — if a product needs a certification the vendor's level doesn't cover, Odoo opens the Compliance Fail Wizard. It's a block-with-override: you either delete the product line or write a fail reason (per line or one general reason) to proceed.
2) Expired certificate — on Confirm, an expired vendor certificate can either warn (you can continue) or block (no confirm button until it's fixed), depending on its setting.

A few words you'll hear

Supplier Level
A simple quality tier — the module ships with three: A, B, C.
Supplier Compliance Type
A level + an industry (e.g. "A Automotive"); this is what you assign to vendors and products.
Included Levels
Lower tiers a compliance type also counts as — so a high tier automatically satisfies lower-tier requirements.
Minimum Supplier Certification
The compliance type(s) a product requires from its supplier. Only products with this set are ever checked.
Certificate
A document on a vendor with an expiry date and a state (Valid / Expiring Soon / Expired); can warn or block on PO confirm.
Compliance Fail Reason
A written justification a buyer records to push an order through despite a mismatch — logged for audits.
