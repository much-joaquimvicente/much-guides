Import & Export — Orders & Products

A developer-level walk-through of how the Shopware connector imports products and orders into Odoo: every phase, every field, and what is mandatory versus optional. Grounded in the actual connector code.

1. The one idea to hold on to

Every import happens in two stages. First the connector fetches raw JSON from the Shopware Admin API and parks it, untouched, in an intermediate "shop" record (its payload field). Then, separately, it processes that payload — mapping the pieces it needs into a real Odoo record.

Why split it? Fetching is decoupled from mapping. If the mapping fails, the fetched data is safe and the record can be reprocessed; each stage runs on its own scheduled job; and every failure is written to the iHub log without losing anything.

Words you'll meet

Payload
The full raw JSON Shopware sent, stored as-is on the staging record. Nothing is thrown away — mapping just reads what it needs from it.
Staging record
An in-between Odoo record (shop.product.template / shop.sale.order) that holds the payload before it becomes a real product or order.
Mapper
A saved link between a Shopware value (a tax, a category, an attribute) and its Odoo equivalent, so the connector knows which Odoo record to use.
Mandatory
Without it, the record is not created — the import stops for that item and waits for you to fix it.

4. What is deliberately NOT imported

Orders skipped at fetch: orders in completed or cancelled state; orders that already have a shipped delivery; anything outside the date window (createdAt since the last import).
Products: only templates are queried (parentId = null); variants arrive nested as children. A record that is itself a child is skipped — its parent creates it.
