★Glossary

👋 Welcome aboard!

Odoo Sales is where selling happens: you build a quotation, send it, the customer accepts, it becomes a sales order, and that drives delivery and invoicing. Almost every integration we build touches Sales, so this flow is worth knowing cold.

🧠 Read this first — the one big idea

One document changes state. A quotation and a sales order are the same record at different stages: Draft → Sent → confirmed Sales Order. Confirming it is the moment a proposal becomes a commitment that feeds Inventory and Accounting.

1 What Sales Is

Turn interest into a priced offer, then into a firm order.

Sales manages the paperwork of selling: quotations (offers), sales orders (accepted offers), and the hand-off to delivery and invoicing. Because it shares products and customers with every other app, a confirmed order automatically ripples out to stock and accounting.

🧾 Quotation

A draft offer you send a customer.

✅ Sales Order

A confirmed, accepted quotation.

📦 Order line

One product + quantity + price on the order.

💶 Invoice

The bill created from the order.

2 Products for Selling

What you put on an order — set up once, reused everywhere.

A product carries the info Sales needs: its sales price, taxes, unit of measure, and its type. The type matters:

📦

Storable

Tracked in Inventory — stock goes up/down.

🧰

Consumable

Bought/sold but stock isn’t tracked.

🛎️

Service

No stock — e.g. consulting hours.

The invoicing policy (ordered vs delivered) decides whether you can bill on order or only after delivery — see Chapter 6.

4 Quotation → Sales Order

The lifecycle, and the moment of commitment.

1Draft (Quotation) — editable proposal, nothing committed.
2Sent — emailed to the customer as a PDF / online quote.
3Sales Order — customer accepts → you Confirm. Now it’s a commitment.
4Locked — finalised so it can’t be changed by mistake.
ℹ️

Customers can accept, e-sign and even pay online from the quotation link — which confirms the order automatically.

5 Pricing — Pricelists & Discounts

Charge the right price to the right customer automatically.

A pricelist is a set of pricing rules — per customer group, currency, quantity or date (e.g. “-10% for wholesalers”, “USD prices”, “bulk discounts”). Assign one to a customer and quotations price themselves. You can also apply a manual discount per order line.

💡

Let pricelists do the work instead of hand-editing prices — it keeps pricing consistent and auditable.

6 From Order to Cash

How a confirmed order becomes a delivery and an invoice.

Confirming an order can create a delivery in Inventory (if it has storable products) and lets you create an invoice in Accounting. When you can invoice depends on the invoicing policy:

Invoicing policyYou can invoice…
Ordered quantitiesAs soon as the order is confirmed.
Delivered quantitiesOnly what has actually been delivered.

Click Create Invoice on the order and it hands off to Accounting (see the Accounting & Invoicing handbook).

7 Customers, Pipeline & Reporting

Who you sell to, where deals come from, and how it’s going.

Customers and contacts are shared across Odoo. Before a quotation, opportunities often live in CRM as leads/opportunities in a pipeline; a won opportunity becomes a quotation. Sales Analysis reporting then shows revenue by product, salesperson, customer and period.

ℹ️

The same customer record ties together their quotations, orders, invoices and support tickets — one 360° view.
