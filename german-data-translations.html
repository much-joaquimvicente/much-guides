German Data & Translations — User Guide

Pre-loaded German accounting/HR master data plus polished German-language translations, so Odoo works the way German businesses and accountants expect. Written for a new team member.

1. What is this?

"German Data & Translations" (internally branded Much OfD — "Odoo für Deutschland") is a pair of add-on modules. One pre-fills your database with German accounting and HR reference data on install; the other replaces Odoo's built-in German text with cleaner, more accurate translations. Together they save you from setting up German-specific data by hand and from living with rough machine-style German labels.

Why it exists: Odoo ships English-first defaults and its standard German translation has gaps. German businesses also need specific local artifacts Odoo does not provide out of the box — the official AfA depreciation tables, German working-time and public-holiday calendars, and the BWA report every tax advisor expects. These modules fill those gaps so a German company can start immediately.

Heads-up: this does not load German states or bank lists. What it actually loads is AfA depreciation asset models, working-time calendars, public holidays and BWA reports (Data module) plus improved German UI translations (Translations module).

What each module provides

Much OfD - German Data
Loads on install: AfA depreciation asset models (by industry), working-time calendars, public holidays, and ready-made BWA financial reports (SKR03 & SKR04).
Much OfD - German Translations
Replaces Odoo's German (de_DE) interface text across many apps with corrected, business-friendly wording — re-applied automatically on every upgrade.

A few words you'll hear

AfA (Absetzung für Abnutzung)
Germany's official rules/tables for depreciating (writing down) the value of business assets over time.
Asset model
A reusable template that defines how a type of asset depreciates; this module pre-creates many, grouped by industry.
BWA (Betriebswirtschaftliche Auswertung)
A standard German monthly management report summarising revenue, costs and profit.
SKR03 / SKR04
The two most common German standard charts of accounts; the BWA ships in a variant for each.
Industry
A category on AfA asset models; selecting industries activates the matching depreciation templates.
