Enhanced Traceability — User Guide

Follow every lot and serial number through manufacturing and repair, so you can prove exactly what went into — and what's been done to — each product. Written for a new team member.

1. What is this?

This module extends Odoo's built-in manufacturing and repair tools so you can track lots and serial numbers all the way through production and after-sales service. It records which parts are currently inside a serialised product, adds repair "service dates" into the standard traceability report, and links helpdesk tickets to the exact product and lot. In short, it makes the paper trail behind a finished product richer and easier to read.

Why it exists: when a product is recalled, fails a quality audit, or a customer disputes a repair, you need to answer "what exactly is inside this unit, and what has been done to it?" — fast and provably. This matters most in regulated or high-value manufacturing (electronics, medical, machinery).

Good to know: despite the "Manufacturing Traceability" name, the extra features focus on the repair & service side — service dates, live "active components", ticket links, and bulk repair tools. It relies on the Repair and Helpdesk apps being installed.

What it adds over standard Odoo

Service Date in the traceability report
A new column showing, for any repair line, when that repair was completed. Scrap lines are hidden to keep the report clean.
Active Components
A live list of the parts currently inside a unit — automatically excluding anything removed or recycled during a past repair.
Helpdesk ticket links
A "Tickets" smart button on product and lot forms, plus Product / Lot fields on the ticket.
Bulk repair tools
Create many repair orders from selected lots, and Confirm / Start / End / Cancel many at once.

A few words you'll hear

Lot / Serial number
The ID for a specific batch (lot) or a single unit (serial); the anchor for all tracking.
Traceability report
Odoo's report showing every stock move for a lot/serial, upstream and downstream.
Upstream / downstream
Upstream = where the material came from; downstream = where it went.
Active components
The parts currently installed in a serialised product, after repairs are accounted for.
Service date
The date a repair was completed — now shown in the traceability report.
