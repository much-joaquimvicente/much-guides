Payment Providers (PayPal) — User Guide

Connect your PayPal Business account to your Odoo shop and offer PayPal, Pay Later, Venmo and card payments — with saved methods and automatic status updates. Written for a new team member.

1. What is this?

This is the much. PayPal suite — a bundle of Odoo modules built with PayPal. It links your PayPal Business account to your Odoo online shop so customers can pay with the PayPal wallet, Pay Later instalments, Venmo, Google Pay, Apple Pay and credit/debit cards. It also handles merchant setup with a guided wizard, lets shoppers securely save payment methods ("vaulting"), and keeps Odoo and PayPal in step automatically using webhooks.

Why it exists: Odoo ships with a basic built-in PayPal option, but it is limited to the classic redirect. This suite gives the full modern experience in one place — no-copy-paste onboarding, extra payment methods, saved methods, on-site "Pay in 4" banners and express-checkout buttons.

What's in the suite

PayPal: Payment Integration
The core engine — connects Odoo to your PayPal Business account and handles payments, refunds and logging.
PayPal: Merchant Configurator
The "Display Options" that let you turn methods on/off and control where banners & buttons appear.
PayPal: Vaulting
Lets logged-in customers save PayPal / Venmo / card details and manage them from their account.
PayPal: Webhook Base
Behind-the-scenes plumbing that safely receives and queues PayPal's status updates.
PayPal: Website Sale
Storefront touches — Pay Later / Venmo banners, express-checkout buttons, shipping.

A few words you'll hear

Payment provider
The configured connection to a payment service (here "PayPal Business Integration").
Onboarding wizard
The guided popup that logs you into PayPal and fills your Merchant/Client ID automatically.
Vaulting
Securely saving a customer's payment method for faster repeat purchases.
Webhook
An automatic message PayPal sends Odoo when something happens (e.g. a payment is captured).
Sandbox vs Production
Sandbox = safe test mode with fake money; Production = live, real payments.
