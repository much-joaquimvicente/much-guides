German Document Templates (DIN 5008) — User Guide

Make your Odoo invoices, quotations, orders and delivery slips print in the layout German business letters are expected to follow. Written for a new team member.

1. What is this?

DIN 5008 is the official German standard for how a business letter is laid out on the page: where the recipient's address goes, where fold marks sit, and where the sender info and "info block" belong. This module (the much QWeb Updater suite) rebuilds Odoo's printed documents to follow that layout, and adds a settings screen where you customise logos, footers, colours and extra text — no coding. It is a suite: one core module plus small extensions for Sales, Accounting, Purchasing and Inventory.

Why it exists: in Germany, business correspondence is expected to follow DIN 5008 — it is what customers, vendors and authorities recognise as "proper". There's a practical payoff too: the recipient address lands exactly in the window of a standard German envelope, and fold marks let the page fold to fit. Odoo's default layout is not DIN-compliant out of the box.

The base A4 grid, fold marks and hole-punch marks come from Odoo's standard l10n_din5008 layout. This suite builds on top of it and adjusts the header, address block, logo, colours and footer.

What it changes on the printed page

Recipient address block
Repositioned to the DIN 5008 window-envelope location.
Header & logo
A resizable logo and a one-line company sender line above the address, in your primary colour.
Line-item table
Table headers recoloured to your company's primary/secondary colours.
Footer
Rebuilt as four equal columns — either your free text, or auto-filled company details, VAT ID and bank account.
Extra fields
Optional "Order Title" and "Description before Table" that print above the item table.

Affected documents: invoices & credit notes, quotations & sales orders, purchase orders & RFQs, and delivery slips (which also gain country of origin, HS codes and weights).

A few words you'll hear

DIN 5008
The German standard defining business-letter formatting (margins, address position, info block, fold marks).
Anschriftfeld (address field)
The fixed zone where the recipient address prints, positioned to show through a window envelope.
Falzmarken (fold marks)
Small ticks in the left margin showing where to fold the page for a DIN envelope.
Info block (Informationsblock)
The right-hand box with document metadata — date, customer number, reference.
QWeb
Odoo's templating engine that renders every printed report; this module edits those templates.
