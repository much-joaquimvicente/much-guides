★Glossary

👋 Welcome aboard!

Odoo Helpdesk is where customer questions and problems become tickets that a team works through to resolution. It brings every channel — email, web form, live chat — into one pipeline, adds deadlines (SLAs) so nothing is forgotten, and measures how happy customers are afterwards.

🧠 Read this first — the one big idea

A ticket is just a task for support. It arrives through a channel, lands in a team, moves across stages until solved, and is answered through the same chatter you already know. Master those four words and Helpdesk is easy.

1 What Helpdesk Is

Turn scattered customer requests into a single, trackable queue.

Instead of support living in someone’s inbox, Helpdesk collects every request as a ticket in a shared pipeline. The team sees what is open, who owns it, how urgent it is and whether it is running late — so problems get solved instead of lost.

🎫 Ticket

One customer request or problem.

👥 Team

A group that handles a category of tickets.

🗂️ Stage

Where the ticket is: New → In Progress → Solved.

⏰ SLA

A promised deadline to respond or resolve.

2 Helpdesk Teams

A team defines who handles which tickets, and how they are shared out.

Helpdesk → Configuration → Teams → New

Each team has its own pipeline and settings. The key choice is the assignment method — how new tickets are given to agents:

✋

Manually

Agents pick tickets from the queue themselves.

🎲

Randomly

Odoo spreads tickets evenly at random.

⚖️

Balanced

Odoo assigns to whoever has the fewest open tickets.

ℹ️

You also set visibility (who can see the team’s tickets) and which channels feed it — covered next.

3 Channels — how tickets arrive

Customers reach you many ways; Helpdesk funnels them all into tickets.

✉️Email alias — customers email e.g. support@company.com and a ticket is created automatically.
🌐Website form — a “Submit a ticket” page on your site.
💬Live chat — a chat widget on the site can spin a conversation into a ticket.
⌨️Manual — an agent creates a ticket by hand (e.g. after a phone call).
💡

Turn channels on per team in the team’s settings. The email alias is the workhorse for most support teams.

5 Stages & Pipeline

Tickets flow across a kanban board just like project tasks.

1New — just arrived, unassigned or unstarted.
2In Progress — an agent is working it.
3Solved — answer given, awaiting confirmation.
4Done / Closed — resolved and put to rest.

Drag tickets between stages as you work. A folded stage (like Done) keeps the board tidy. You can merge duplicate tickets so one conversation holds the whole story.

6 SLA Policies

A promise to answer within a set time — measured automatically.

An SLA (Service Level Agreement) policy sets a target like “High-priority tickets reach Solved within 8 working hours”. Odoo tracks the clock and colours the ticket: on time or breached. Policies can depend on priority, tags or team.

Helpdesk → Configuration → SLA Policies
ℹ️

SLAs measure in working hours, not wall-clock time — so a Friday-evening ticket isn’t unfairly “late” by Monday.

7 Working a Ticket & Reporting

Answer through the chatter, and learn from the numbers.

Reply to the customer with Send message; keep colleague notes as Log note (the same message/note rule from Getting Started — get this right so customers never see internal chatter). If enabled, log time on the ticket via a Timesheets tab. Close when resolved.

Satisfaction & reports

⭐ Ratings

After closing, customers rate the help — great/okay/bad.

⏰ SLA Success

Share of tickets that met their deadline.

📊 Ticket Analysis

Volume, resolution time and load by team/agent.

📚 Help Center

Publish Knowledge articles so customers self-serve.

★ Plain-English glossary

The words you'll keep hearing — decoded.

Ticket
A single customer request or problem tracked to resolution.
Team
A group handling a category of tickets, with its own pipeline.
Assignment method
How new tickets are shared out: manual, random or balanced.
Channel
A way tickets arrive: email, web form, live chat or manual.
Stage
A kanban column showing a ticket’s progress.
SLA
A promised response/resolution deadline, tracked automatically.
Priority
A star rating that ranks urgency and can drive SLAs.
Merge
Combining duplicate tickets into one.
Rating
A customer’s satisfaction score after a ticket closes.
Help Center
A public site of Knowledge articles for customer self-service.
