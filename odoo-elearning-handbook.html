★Glossary

👋 Welcome aboard!

Odoo eLearning lets you build online courses from videos, documents and quizzes. We use it to onboard new joiners and to teach customers how our products work. Learners watch, read, take quizzes and earn certificates — and you can see exactly how far each person has got.

🧠 Read this first — the one big idea

A course is a list of content grouped into sections. Content can be a video, a document, a web page or a quiz. Set who can enrol, optionally add a certification, publish it — and Odoo tracks every learner’s progress.

1 What eLearning Is

Package knowledge into courses people work through at their own pace.

eLearning is Odoo’s course builder. You assemble lessons, decide who can take the course, and publish it on your website. Learners progress through the content while Odoo records completion — perfect for repeatable training you don’t want to deliver live every time.

🎓 Course

A complete lesson set on one topic.

🧱 Content

A single lesson: video, document, page or quiz.

📑 Section

A group of contents inside a course.

🏅 Certification

A graded quiz that awards a certificate.

2 Courses

The container for a topic’s lessons.

eLearning → New (course)

Give the course a title, a description, tags and a cover image. Inside, organise lessons into sections (e.g. “Basics”, “Advanced”) so long courses stay tidy. Reorder content by dragging.

3 Content Types

Mix formats to keep courses engaging.

🎬

Video

An embedded or uploaded video lesson.

📄

Document

A PDF or slide deck to read.

🌐

Web page

A rich page written right in Odoo.

❓

Quiz

Questions that test and reinforce learning.

Add content inside a course and pick its type. Each item can require the learner to finish it before the next unlocks, if you want a strict path.

4 Access & Enrolment

Decide who can take the course and how they get in.

Enrol policyMeaning
Public / FreeAnyone can take it.
On invitationOnly people you invite can join.
On paymentLearners buy access (ties into eCommerce).

Course visibility controls who even sees it exists (everyone, signed-in members, or invited only). Combine visibility + enrol policy to make courses public, members-only or paid.

5 Quizzes & Certification

Check understanding and hand out proof of completion.

A quiz is content made of questions, each worth points. A certification course requires passing a quiz to earn a certificate the learner can share. Great for “you must pass this to be signed off” training.

💡

Keep quizzes short and focused on the must-know points — they reinforce learning as much as they test it.

6 Gamification & Community

Points and badges that make learning stick.

⭐ Karma

Points learners earn for finishing content and passing quizzes.

🏅 Badges

Awards for milestones — recognition that motivates.

🥇 Ranks

Levels reached as karma grows.

💬 Reviews & Forum

Learners can rate courses and ask questions.

ℹ️

Gamification is optional — turn it on when you want engagement, leave it off for straightforward internal training.

7 Publishing & Reporting

Put the course live, then watch progress.

Courses live on your website. Toggle Published to make a course visible; unpublish to hide a draft. Once live, reporting shows attendees, how far each has progressed, and completion/quiz results — so you know who has actually done the training.

💡

For onboarding, check the attendee list to confirm new joiners have completed the required courses.
