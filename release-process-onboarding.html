much. release process — onboarding

What a release is, when they happen, how it ships, and how we tell the world — for Product Owners & Support.

1. The big picture

A release is a tagged version of a much. module (e.g. v18.0.1.2.0) bundling everything merged and QA-tested since the last version, then shipped to customers. It doesn't run on a fixed calendar — it's cut when enough fixes and features have piled up on the QA branch, or for an urgent fix. The dashed block is the customer-email step that doesn't exist yet — a future project.

2. The four phases

The process runs left to right through the three phases you care about — the technical prep, publishing, and announcing — plus the future customer-email step (dashed, not built yet).
