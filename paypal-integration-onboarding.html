much. PayPal Official Integration — onboarding process map

How much.'s PayPal payment suite connects Odoo to PayPal — for a new engineer, support, or PM. Every step is grounded in the actual code, with one named example running through each workflow.

1. The big picture

The suite sits between Odoo and PayPal. A merchant connects their PayPal Business account once (no copy-pasting keys); after that, every shopper payment, refund, saved card and webhook flows through a small set of reusable building blocks. Two things live outside Odoo: PayPal's v2 REST API, and a much.-owned Google Cloud service that hands out the partner-level credentials so they are never stored in the Odoo database.

2. The architecture

The suite is six Odoo modules layered on Odoo's core payment framework. payment_paypal_suite is a meta-installer that pulls in the other five (it is set to auto-install). The core is payment_paypal_integration — provider, onboarding, the PayPal v2 Orders API, refunds and the secret manager. The other modules bolt on capabilities: reliable webhooks, merchant display config, vaulting, and the storefront / express-checkout experience.

1Password also holds two kinds of sandbox test accounts, and the naming tells you which side of the flow each one is for:

"merchant" accounts
Simulate the merchant side — logging in during onboarding and configuring the provider (workflows 0 and 6).
"buyer" accounts
Simulate the shopper flow on the merchant's website — checkout, express checkout, and vaulting (workflows 1–3).

Typical loop: put the provider in Sandbox, onboard with a merchant test account, then shop as a buyer test account, and watch the Queue (webhook events) and Logs (API calls) smart buttons on the provider form to confirm what happened.
