👋 Welcome aboard!

Odoo Website is a drag-and-drop site builder, and eCommerce makes that site sell. Because the shop uses the same products, stock and customers as the rest of Odoo, an online order flows straight into Sales, Inventory and Accounting — no separate system to reconcile. This underpins our Shopware, Shopify and ChannelEngine work.

🧠 Read this first — the one big idea

The shop is just your Odoo products, published to a web page. Build pages by dragging building blocks, tick a product as Published, and it appears in the shop — where a sale becomes a normal Odoo sales order. One database, storefront included.

1 What Website & eCommerce Is

A website builder and an online shop, sharing one database with everything else.

Website lets you build and edit pages visually — no code. eCommerce adds a storefront, cart and checkout on top, selling the products you already manage in Odoo. Every online order is a real Odoo sales order, so stock and accounting update automatically.

🌐 Website

The pages, built from drag-and-drop blocks.

🛒 Shop

The product catalogue customers browse.

🧺 Cart & Checkout

Where a visitor buys and pays.

👤 Portal

A customer’s account and order history.

2 The Website Builder

Design pages by dragging blocks — what you see is what visitors get.

Click Edit to enter build mode, then drag building blocks (text, images, columns, banners, pricing, forms) onto the page and tweak them in the side panel. Save to keep changes; toggle Published to make a page public or keep it a draft.

✏️Edit — enter build mode.
🧱Drag blocks — drop in sections and style them.
💾Save — store your changes.
🌍Publish — make it live for visitors.

3 Products Online

Getting an existing Odoo product into the shop.

Open a product and set it Published to list it in the shop. The product page shows images, description, price and an Add to Cart button. Variants (size, colour) let one product page offer multiple options.

ℹ️

Good images and descriptions sell — the product page is your storefront window.

4 Shop & Categories

Help customers find things fast.

The shop page lists published products. eCommerce categories group them into a browsable menu (e.g. Men / Women / Accessories), and filters and sorting let shoppers narrow by attribute or price. You can arrange and resize product tiles right on the page.

6 Customer Accounts & Promotions

Repeat customers and incentives to buy.

Shoppers can create an account to see their order history and documents through the customer portal. To drive sales, use discounts, coupon/promo codes and pricelists — the same pricing engine as Sales, applied online.

🎟️ Coupons

One-off codes for a discount.

🏷️ Promotions

Rules like “buy 2 get 1”, auto-applied.

💷 Pricelists

Customer/quantity/currency-based online pricing.

👤 Portal

Self-service account and order history.

7 SEO & Reporting

Get found on Google, and see what sells.

Each page has SEO settings — title, description and keywords — so search engines index it well. Sales made online appear in normal Sales reporting, and eCommerce adds views for online-specific metrics (abandoned carts, top products).

ℹ️

Because online orders are just Odoo sales orders, all your usual Sales and Accounting reports already include them — no separate reconciliation.
