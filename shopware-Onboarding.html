Shopware ↔ Odoo — User Guide

A plain-language guide for new team members. No technical background needed — start with the basics, then click through what happens, what you need to do, and where to find things.

1. What is this integration?

Shopware is your online shop — where customers browse and buy. Odoo is the ERP system that runs the business behind it — orders, stock, invoices and deliveries. On their own, the two don't talk to each other.

The integration (or "connector") is a little messenger that sits between them and keeps both sides in step, on its own. So when a customer buys something, or you change a price, the messenger carries it across — nobody has to copy it by hand.

A few words you'll hear

Import
Bringing information into Odoo (for example, an order from the shop).
Export
Sending information out to Shopware (for example, a new stock number).
Sync
Keeping both sides showing the same thing.
Scheduled action
A task that runs automatically on a timer (usually once a day), so you don't have to start it.
Log
A diary the system keeps, so you can see what happened and why — your first place to look if something seems off.
Integration Hub
The menu in Odoo where all of this lives (Integration Hub > Shopware).

2. What happens automatically vs what you do
